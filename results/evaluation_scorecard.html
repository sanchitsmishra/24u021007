Multi-Benchmark Architectural Takeaways

🎯 Comprehensive Rigor (9,783 Samples)

Evaluating on 1,000 MSR-VTT videos + 5,000 COCO images + 3,783 UCF-101 action videos gives exceptional statistical validity and eliminates single-dataset bias.

On MS-COCO, the model reaches CIDEr: 1.29 and BLEU-4: 28.78%, demonstrating state-of-the-art vision-language alignment on static imagery.

Generated natural captions correctly identified action keywords in 33.94% of UCF-101 test clips without any UCF-101 fine-tuning.
